### [Warm-Start Quantum Computation for Energy Readout and State Preparation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12485)

Tien D. Kieu

量子算法 · 量子模拟 · 量子测量 · 量子应用 · 化学新材料

**中文摘要**

估计基态能量与制备基态都可分为一个前端和一个后端：前端提供一个近似态 $|\chi\rangle$，后端读取或精炼它。我们论证并在每一步测量：跨越这一分工的唯一对象，是前端在问题哈密顿量 $H_P$ 的本征态上的谱权重分布 $p_n=|\langle E_n|\chi\rangle|^2$。下游的一切都以该分布计价，因此前端是一个可替换模块；我们在不改变其右侧任何东西的情况下将其替换了七次。我们称这一安排为 WARP。

四个后端消耗该分布。一个读出后端（A）在器件上采样 $g(t)=\langle\chi|e^{-iH_Pt}|\chi\rangle$，并通过经典信号处理提取真实本征值，从比傅里叶变换所需短 $50\times$ 的信号中返回一个低于其所接收态变分界的能量。三条路线则把同一账户花在一个态上。绝热调度（B）的最小能隙由重叠设定；离散放大（C）在深度上更便宜，但必须在由重叠确定的索引处停止，因此它需要一个测量值而非一个界；深度为 $\mathcal{O}(3^m)$ 的递归（D）则提出它究竟能否运行的问题。

这些相互作用才是实质。在拉伸的 N$_2$ 上，基于采样的对角化报告了一个高 79 mHa 的变分界，超出化学精度五十倍；读取它已经制备好的态可将 $E_0$ 返回到 $1.4\times10^{-6}$ Ha，进入化学精度以内三个数量级，且不需要新的前端和新的电路。并且，由于权重为一切定价，它们在稀少时也必须可测量，因此我们将弱参考与强参考进行干涉，并恢复了比散粒噪声底低四个数量级的重叠。我们全程绘制工作包络，并说明其局限。

**研究结论（依据正文结论节选与摘要）**

该工作表明，预热启动的核心资源是问题哈密顿量本征基上的谱权重分布 $p_n=|\langle E_n|\chi\rangle|^2$，而非前端身份或单一态质量标量；基态权重 $p_0$ 决定所考虑制备过程的主导重叠依赖，其余谱权重决定分辨谱的难度。基于同一预热态的 WARP 架构可支持谱读出与态制备，并在拉伸 N$_2$ 等分子模拟中显示：对已制备态做谱读出可把能量恢复到约 $1.4\times10^{-6}$ Ha，而变分界约高 79 mHa；SO$_2$ 例子报告了 0.033 Ha 的改进。作者强调这些比较不构成对经典方法的普适量子优势，WARP 也不应被解读为某一后端普适更优；结论受超分辨不增加可分辨谱线数、参数化读出需合适模型阶数与谱覆盖、递归/放大依赖有用重叠、WA-ASP 需可实现的投影态制备以及模拟未包含完整硬件噪声等限制。

**创新点**

- 把预热启动资源刻画为问题哈密顿量本征基上的谱权重分布 $p_n=|\langle E_n|\chi\rangle|^2$，并据此提出前端可替换、后端消耗同一资源的 WARP 架构。
- 用 ESPRIT 对 $g(t)=\langle\chi|e^{-iH_Pt}|\chi\rangle$ 做参数化谱估计，在测试模拟中以约短 $50\times$ 的信号达到化学精度；并指出分次采集两个 Hadamard 测试正交分量会引入共轭谱分量、交错采集可消除该失效模式。
- 展示同一预热态可由绝热调度、离散振幅放大和递归双括号虚时演化等制备后端消耗，并用干涉计量恢复低于散粒噪声底四个数量级的重叠。

<summary>原文摘要</summary>

Estimating a ground-state energy and preparing a ground state both split into a front-end that supplies an approximate state $|χ\rangle$ and a back-end that reads or refines it. We argue, and measure at every step, that the only thing crossing that split is the front-end's spectral weight distribution $p_n=|\langle E_n|χ\rangle|^2$ over the eigenstates of the problem Hamiltonian $H_P$. Everything downstream is priced in it, so the front-end is a swappable module, and we swap it seven times without altering anything to its right. We call the arrangement WARP.

A readout (A) samples $g(t)=\langleχ|e^{-iH_Pt}|χ\rangle$ on the device and extracts true eigenvalues by classical signal processing, returning an energy below the variational bound of the state it was handed, from a signal $50\times$ shorter than a Fourier transform needs. On stretched N$_2$ a sample-based diagonalisation reports a variational bound 79 mHa high, fifty times outside chemical accuracy; reading the state it has already prepared returns $E_0$ to $1.4\times10^{-6}$ Ha, three orders of magnitude inside it, and needs no new front-end and no new circuit. And because the weights price everything, they must be measurable when scarce, so we beat a weak reference against a strong one and recover overlaps four orders of magnitude below the shot-noise floor. We map the operating envelope throughout, and state the limitations.

### [Exact solutions of non-Markovian dissipative Ising models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12546)

Mohammad Maghrebi

开放量子系统 · 量子多体物理

**中文摘要**

量子器件与量子模拟器的应用需要准确理解其动力学以及环境噪声的影响。虽然某些噪声源可以建模为马尔可夫耗散，但与谐振器或振动模式的耦合也常常产生非马尔可夫动力学，这使得系统难以表征，尤其是对于相互作用的多体系统。在这里，我们确定了一大类同时具有非马尔可夫动力学和马尔可夫耗散的可精确求解多体量子系统。我们考虑一个原型模型：自旋与有损耗的玻色模式耦合，而这些玻色模式介导伊辛型相互作用。我们排除横向场，但允许局域自旋耗散，这种耗散不必与相互作用对易，包括退相位、弛豫、泵浦，甚至产生相干性的过程。利用精确的随机表示，我们将多体动力学解耦为单个自旋的演化，从而在不绝热消去玻色子的情况下得到精确的自旋可观测量和关联。在马尔可夫极限下，我们的解描述了具有关联退相位和非互易相互作用的伊辛模型。一个自旋压缩的例子表明，可实现的压缩以及其最优制备时间都强烈依赖于非马尔可夫动力学与马尔可夫耗散之间的相互作用。

**研究结论（依据摘要）**

摘要报告：作者确定了一大类同时具有非马尔可夫动力学与马尔可夫耗散的可精确求解多体量子系统，所考虑模型为自旋与有损耗玻色模式耦合、由玻色模式介导伊辛型相互作用，且不含横向场并允许局域自旋耗散；借助精确随机表示可将多体动力学解耦为单个自旋演化，在不绝热消去玻色子的情况下给出精确自旋可观测量和关联。该结果适用于此类模型及其马尔可夫极限，后者描述具有关联退相位和非互易相互作用的伊辛模型；自旋压缩例子表明可实现的压缩及其最优制备时间强烈依赖于非马尔可夫动力学与马尔可夫耗散的相互作用。上述为摘要中的主张，未提供正文结论章节。

**创新点**

- 确定了一大类同时具有非马尔可夫动力学和马尔可夫耗散的可精确求解多体量子系统。
- 利用精确随机表示将多体动力学解耦为单个自旋的演化，从而在不绝热消去玻色子的情况下得到精确的自旋可观测量和关联。
- 在马尔可夫极限下，所得解描述了具有关联退相位和非互易相互作用的伊辛模型。

<summary>原文摘要</summary>

Applications of quantum devices and simulators require an accurate understanding of their dynamics as well as the effects of environmental noise. While some sources of noise can be modeled as Markovian dissipation, coupling to resonators or vibrational modes often produces non-Markovian dynamics as well, making it difficult to characterize the system, particularly for interacting many-body systems. Here, we identify a broad class of exactly solvable many-body quantum systems with both non-Markovian dynamics and Markovian dissipation. We consider a prototypical model of spins coupled to lossy bosonic modes that mediate Ising-type interactions. We exclude a transverse field but allow local spin dissipation that need not commute with the interactions, including dephasing, relaxation, pumping, and even processes that generate coherences. Using an exact stochastic representation, we decouple the many-body dynamics into the evolution of individual spins, yielding exact spin observables and correlations without adiabatically eliminating the bosons. In the Markovian limit, our solutions describe Ising models with correlated dephasing and nonreciprocal interactions. An example of spin squeezing shows that both the achievable squeezing and its optimal preparation time depend strongly on the interplay between non-Markovian dynamics and Markovian dissipation.

### [Engineering Ergodicity-Breaking and Stable Emergent Conservation Laws in a Generic Quantum Many-body System Under Strong Periodic Drive](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12551)

Sagnik Chaudhuri、Anirban Das、Asmi Haldar、Arnab Das

量子多体物理 · 量子控制

**中文摘要**

一个周期性驱动的量子多体系统被认为会按照遍历性假设趋近于局域无穷温度类状态，前提是所有耦合/参数都处于同一量级。当驱动强于某一阈值时，一大类系统被证明会打破这一要求，表现出动力学冻结（Dynamical Freezing, DF）：由于出现了对其初始值具有永久记忆的局域算符，遍历性被破坏（PRX 11, 021008, 2021）。然而，近期在一个特定设置中注意到了DF的一个例外（PRX 16, 011041, 2026）。在这里，我们确定了强驱动下系统发生遍历性破缺的条件，并展示了如何通过精确的局域工程来稳定DF。我们进一步表明，在非DF系统中，按自旋翻转排序时，Floquet哈密顿量 $H_{eff} = (i/T)\ln{[U(T)]}$ 是非局域的；而在我们简单的局域DF工程下，它变为准局域，同时哈密顿量 $H(t)$ 在每个 $t$ 时刻仍不可积。这种令人困惑的非普适性在平衡态中并不存在；在平衡态中，不可积性被认为普遍意味着遍历性和热化。我们的工作为在各种量子平台上工程化新型遍历性破缺的相互作用Floquet物相开辟了道路，并且还赋予了DF辅助的量子误差缓解和态稳定技术（见 Nature 653, 1027 (2026)）前所未有的通用性和灵活性。

**研究结论（依据摘要）**

摘要报告：作者识别出强周期驱动量子多体系统中遍历性破缺的条件，并给出通过精确局域工程设计稳定动力学冻结（DF）的方法；在所考虑的按自旋翻转排序的Floquet哈密顿量中，非DF系统里 $H_{eff}$ 表现为非局域，而在该局域DF工程下转为准局域，同时每个时刻的 $H(t)$ 仍不可积。作者据此强调这种非普适性在平衡态中不出现，并认为该方案可用于在多种量子平台工程化遍历性破缺的Floquet物相，以及辅助量子误差缓解与态稳定。上述结论的适用范围限于摘要所述的强驱动、局域工程设定。

**创新点**

- 识别出强驱动量子多体系统中遍历性破缺的条件。
- 提出通过精确的局域工程设计来稳定动力学冻结（DF）的方法。
- 揭示Floquet哈密顿量在非DF系统中按自旋翻转排序时非局域、而在该DF工程下转为准局域，同时保持 $H(t)$ 在每个时刻不可积。

<details>
<summary>原文摘要</summary>

A periodically driven quantum many-body system is believed to approach a locally infinite-temperature-like state as dictated by the ergodicity hypothesis, when all the couplings/parameters are of the same order. When the drive is stronger than a threshold value, a large class of systems is shown to break this mandate, exhibiting Dynamical Freezing (DF): ergodicity is broken due to the emergence of local operators with permanent memory of their initial values (PRX 11, 021008, 2021). However, an exception to DF has recently been noted in a particular setting (PRX 16, 011041, 2026). Here we identify the condition for ergodicity-breaking in a system under strong drive, and show how to stabilize DF by exact, local engineering. We further show that the Floquet Hamiltonian $H_{eff} = (i/T)\ln{[U(T)]}$ is non-local when ordered by spin-flips, in a non-DF system, while it turns quasi-local under our simple, local DF engineering that keeps the Hamiltonian $H(t)$ still non-integrable at each $t$. This perplexing non-universality is absent in equilibrium, where non-integrability is believed to imply ergodicity and thermalization universally. Our work paves a way to engineering novel ergodicity-broken interacting Floquet phases of matter in various quantum platforms, and also endows DF-assisted quantum error mitigation and state stabilization techniques (see Nature 653, 1027 (2026)) with unprecedented versatility and flexibility.

### [Correlated Pair Creation with Positive- and Negative-Frequency Oscillators in a Trapped-Ion Quantum Simulator](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12579)

Visal So、Mingjian Zhu、Han Pu、Guido Pagano

量子模拟 · 离子阱 · 量子纠缠

**中文摘要**

在囚禁离子自旋-玻色量子模拟器中，自旋依赖力能够独立控制玻色运动模式的有效频率及其与自旋的耦合强度。我们表明，这些有效频率的相对正负号为设计不同的多模动力学提供了一种重要的可编程资源。聚焦于双模量子 Rabi 模型，将两个模式的频率从同号变为反号会重组自旋介导的玻色相互作用的共振结构，使主导共振过程从激发交换转变为关联对产生。在色散区，由此产生的有效相互作用实现了一个双模参量放大器，具有稳定的振荡区、临界区和指数放大区，并由一个依赖自旋的不稳定性阈值分隔。在这些区域中，动力学产生集体正交压缩和双分运动纠缠。将自旋制备在相干叠加态中，还能使不同自旋分量经历不同的稳定性区域，产生三分自旋-玻色纠缠；而自旋后选择可以相干地组合条件玻色分支，产生非高斯纠缠玻色态。最后，我们概述了利用当前囚禁离子平台可及参数的一种实现方案。

**研究结论（依据正文结论节选与摘要）**

材料表明，作者将有效玻色频率的相对正负号确立为囚禁离子多模自旋-玻色模拟中可独立控制的参数：在双模量子 Rabi 模型中，翻转一个模式的符号可将共振自旋介导相互作用从激发交换变为对产生，并产生阈值依赖自旋条件色散位移的动力学不稳定性，进而生成双模压缩和运动纠缠；相干自旋叠加可使不同自旋分支处于不同稳定性区域，自旋后选择可将条件动力学转化为非高斯纠缠玻色态。作者指出这些效应可用现有囚禁离子实验的参数和控制实现，并讨论了超出色散区、更多模式与自旋以及开放系统等扩展；上述结论主要限于作者所讨论的模型与模拟方案。

**创新点**

- 将有效玻色频率的相对正负号作为可独立编程的参数，用于设计多模自旋-玻色动力学。
- 在双模量子 Rabi 模型中，通过将两个模式从同号改为反号，使主导共振过程从激发交换转为关联对产生，并在色散区实现具有自旋依赖不稳定阈值的双模参量放大器。
- 利用自旋相干叠加和自旋后选择，产生三分自旋-玻色纠缠以及非高斯纠缠玻色态。

<summary>原文摘要</summary>

Spin-dependent forces enable independent control over the effective frequencies of bosonic motional modes and their coupling strengths to spins in trapped-ion spin-boson quantum simulators. We show that the relative signs of these effective frequencies provide an important programmable resource for engineering distinct multimode dynamics. Focusing on a two-mode quantum Rabi model, changing the frequencies of the two modes from the same sign to opposite signs reorganizes the resonance structure of the spin-mediated bosonic interaction, converting the dominant resonant process from excitation exchange to correlated pair creation. Finally, we outline an implementation using parameters within reach of current trapped-ion platforms.

### [Error correction and quantum information spreading on sparse random graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12597)

Quantum mechanics seems to be an incredibly delicate theory. Slight alterations to nearly any part of its structure can lead to huge qualitative differences and wildly unphysical predictions. Similar to the action of Maxwell's demon, the simple act of observation seems to extract entropy from a closed system. For $b<1$, the bias is reversed, generically scouring out long-wavelength fluctuations to produce a hyperuniform gas. We study this effect in a one-dimensional quantum gas, where these two phases are derived analytically for a particular measurement scheme and corroborated numerically for more general procedures with discrete-time projective-measurement circuit models using matrix product state simulations. We additionally discuss how this bias manifests in a common technique that often employs this warped rule, the ``replica trick,'' implying qualitative differences between finite-replica and replica-limit calculations.

### [An entangling Hubbard quasiparticle collider on a digital quantum processor](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13946)

**中文摘要**

Scattering of quasiparticles provides direct access to the interactions that govern strongly correlated systems. In this work, we realize the quasiparticle scattering experiment of the Fermi-Hubbard model on a digital quantum processor by colliding a doublon and a holon in the half-filled chain of 21 sites encoded on 42 qubits of the ibm_boston processor. Beyond the scattering probabilities, the collision generates entanglement by superposing the transmitted and reflected components, which we infer from the particle-number fluctuation. In sharp contrast, a control collision between two doublons shows neither the interaction dependence nor the number fluctuation, as expected for indistinguishable particles. Our work demonstrates that the ability to create quasiparticles of a chosen flavor on demand and program their motion and interactions makes a digital quantum processor a new experimental toolset for probing the entanglement structure generated by quasiparticle scattering.
