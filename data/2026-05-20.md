### [1] [Statistical Quantum Phase Estimation: Extensions and Practical Considerations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18876)
*Amit Surana,Brandon Allen*

Main category: quant-ph

TL;DR: 本文提出了统计量子相位估计(SQPE)框架的多项改进，包括推广随机编译程序以处理负Pauli权重、使用变点检测方法估计基态能量（无需重叠下界估计），以及利用傅里叶级数对称性将电路运行次数减半，并在Qiskit量子模拟器上验证了这些改进。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 标准SQPE方法假设Hamiltonian的Pauli权重为正，且需要估计试验态与真实基态之间重叠的下界，这两个条件在实际中难以满足，限制了其应用。

Method: 1) 将随机编译程序推广到负Pauli权重情形；2) 采用变点检测方法确定累积分布函数的首个跳跃点，从而估计基态能量，无需依赖重叠下界；3) 利用傅里叶级数对称性将所需样本数减少2倍。

Result: 在Qiskit量子模拟器上通过数值实验验证了这些改进方法的有效性，在保持基态能量估计精度的同时，放宽了实际应用中的限制条件并降低了采样成本。

Conclusion: 这些改进使SQPE方法更适用于早期容错量子计算机，提升了其实用性和可操作性，特别是在处理负Pauli权重和无需先验重叠信息方面具有显著优势。

Abstract: We present several refinements and extensions of the statistical quantum phase estimation (SQPE) framework to address some of its key practical limitations, improving its applicability to realistic cases. Recently, a family of statistical approaches for QPE have been proposed where each run uses only a few ancillae and shorter circuits than standard QPE and thus is better suited for early fault-tolerant quantum computers that are qubit-and depth-limited. SQPE method within that family estimates the cumulative distribution function (CDF) associated with spectral density of the Hamiltonian for a given trial state by using its Fourier approximation and then identifies the first jump discontinuity of the CDF to determine the ground state energy (GSE) of the Hamiltonian. It relies on random compilation procedure based on linear combination of unitaries (LCU) decomposition of the Hamiltonian assuming positive Pauli weights and requires a good estimate of lower bound on the overlap between the trial and true ground state, both of which may be difficult to achieve in practice. We address these limitations by generalizing the random compilation procedure for negative Pauli weights and employing a changepoint detection method for determining GSE which does not rely on an estimate of this overlap. We also show that by exploiting symmetry of the Fourier series one can reduce number of circuit runs/samples by a factor of 2x while keeping the GSE estimation accuracy the same. We illustrate these new developments numerically via a quantum simulator in Qiskit.

### [2] [Assisted quantum teleportation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18896)
*Mithilesh Kumar,Kaavya Iyer*

Main category: quant-ph

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种"辅助量子隐形传态"框架，通过第三方（银行）提供多体纠缠资源，可以将非最大纠缠量子对恢复为完美贝尔对，从而实现确定性单位保真度传输。文章分析了银行测量模型和转移模型两种操作角色，给出了GHZ类和W类辅助资源的确定性恢复可行区域，并研究了概率恢复的有限次最优成功概率。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 非最大纠缠量子对（如$\ket{ψ(θ)}_{AB}=\cosθ\ket{00}+\sinθ\ket{11}$）在量子隐形传态中会引入噪声信道，无法实现确定性单位保真度传输，除非$θ=π/4$（即最大纠缠情况）。因此需要引入辅助资源来恢复完美贝尔对，实现无噪声传输。

Method: 提出了辅助量子隐形传态框架，引入第三方"银行"提供辅助多体纠缠资源。分析了两种操作模型：（1）银行测量模型——银行对辅助系统进行测量并广播结果；（2）转移模型——银行将其子系统转移给用户后离开。针对GHZ类和W类辅助纠缠资源，推导了确定性恢复的可行区域，并对概率恢复情况研究了有限次最优成功概率，将银行测量模型的可行性表述为极小极大优化问题。

Result: 给出了GHZ类和W类辅助资源下确定性恢复的显式可行区域；发现W类资源存在操作不等价性（两种模型表现不同）；给出了概率恢复的有限次最优成功概率表征；将银行测量模型的一般纯态辅助资源可行性问题转化为极小极大优化问题。

Conclusion: 辅助量子隐形传态框架有效解决了非最大纠缠资源下的噪声问题。GHZ类和W类辅助资源在两种操作模型下表现出不同的可行区域，尤其是W类资源展现出操作不等价性。该框架为利用辅助纠缠资源提升量子通信保真度提供了系统性的理论工具。

Abstract: Teleportation through a non-maximally entangled pair, e.g., $\ket{ψ(θ)}_{AB}=\cosθ\ket{00}+\sinθ\ket{11}$, induces a noisy channel and cannot achieve deterministic unit-fidelity transmission unless $θ=π/4$. We introduce a framework of \emph{assisted quantum teleportation} in which a third party (the ``Bank'') supplies auxiliary multipartite entanglement to restore a perfect Bell pair on the original $AB$ registers. We analyze two operational roles for the Bank: a Bank-measures model (measurement and broadcast) and a transfer model (the Bank transfers its subsystem and then leaves). For GHZ-class and W-class assistance we derive explicit feasibility regions for deterministic restoration and show an operational inequivalence for W resources. We further characterize finite-shot optimal success probabilities for probabilistic restoration and formulate Bank-measures feasibility for general pure Bank resources as a minimax optimization.

### [3] [Clifford symmetries in quantum many-body systems](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.18966)

<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 对称性的发现对于理解模型并最终解析或数值求解模型至关重要，但寻找对称性通常极其复杂，依赖于人类洞察力。本文旨在用算法补充人类智慧，自动化这一过程。

Method: 利用经典高效的Clifford群，通过图表示来寻找任意多体哈密顿量的对称性。

Result: 在随机和物理哈密顿量上进行了演示，实例涉及多达1000个量子比特。

Conclusion: 该方法能够为模型提供更深入的理解。

Abstract: Obtaining the symmetries of a model is a critical step towards developing an understanding and ultimately analytically or numerically solving the model. However, finding symmetries is generally extremely complicated, often being the result of insightful thinking. In this work, we complement human ingenuity with an algorithm. We leverage the classically efficient Clifford group to find symmetries for arbitrary many-body Hamiltonians via a graph representation. We demonstrate our method on random and physical Hamiltonians, with instances of up to one thousand qubits and demonstrate how our approach can provide deeper understanding of the model.

### [4] [Entangling gate performance and fidelity limits with neutral atom Förster resonances](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19245)

Main category: quant-ph

TL;DR: 本文研究了近Förster共振下中性原子纠缠门的双本征态模型，发现通过耦合共振中的两个对态，门保真度存在上限F≤1-(π/2)/(Vτ_R)，并构造了达到该上限的协议，在现有基础上提升约40%保真度，同时将预测保真度提高最多两个数量级。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统中性原子纠缠门分析仅使用单个有效里德伯对态，但在近Förster共振区域，对态流形中存在共振耦合相互作用通道，改变了控制景观和可实现的保真度，需要更精确的模型来描述这一物理机制。

Method: 1) 建立双本征态模型描述近Förster共振下的耦合通道；2) 推导出门保真度的理论上限表达式；3) 构造在大切比雪夫频率极限下饱和该上限的门协议；4) 评估常见门协议在近Förster共振下的性能。

Result: 1) 推导出保真度上限F≤1-(π/2)/(Vτ_R)；2) 构建的协议在该上限下提升约40%保真度；3) 保留交换动力学后，预测保真度比早期处理提高最多两个数量级。

Conclusion: 近Förster共振下考虑双态耦合可显著提高中性原子纠缠门的保真度，为优化量子门设计提供了新的理论框架和实用协议。

Abstract: Neutral-atom entangling gates are commonly analyzed with a single effective Rydberg-pair state, but near Förster resonances the pair manifold contains resonantly coupled interaction channels that change both the control landscape and the achievable fidelity. We develop a two-eigenstate model for this regime and show that when allowing for coupling to both pair states in the resonance, the gate fidelity is bounded by $\mathcal{F}\leq 1-(π/2)/(Vτ_R)$, for interaction strength $V$ and Rydberg lifetime $τ_R$. We construct a gate protocol that saturates this bound in the large-Rabi-frequency limit, improving the existing fidelity limit by approximately $40\%$. We also evaluate common gate protocols near Förster resonances and find that retaining the exchange dynamics increases predicted fidelities by up to two orders of magnitude over earlier treatments.

### [6] [Unveiling Energetic Advantage in Superconducting Cat-Qubits Quantum Computation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19854)
*Pedro Ramos,Marco Pezzutto,Yasser Omar*

Main category: quant-ph

TL;DR: 本文分析了超导猫量子比特平台上半经典量子傅里叶变换的能耗，研究了量子纠错对能耗的影响，开发了以最小化能耗为目标的参数优化方法，并与经典计算机进行了能效对比，发现超过26量子比特时可能出现量子能量优势。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统上量子计算研究以时间效率（计算速度）为核心关注点，但随着量子系统规模扩大，能效正成为日益重要的考量因素，尤其是量子能效优势（quantum energetic advantage）的可能性被提出后，需要对量子计算的实际能耗进行系统分析。

Method: 在超导猫量子比特平台上，对半经典量子傅里叶变换（Semiclassical Quantum Fourier Transform）进行能耗建模与分析，将量子纠错机制纳入能量估算，开发参数优化方法以在保证量子比特保真度高于阈值的前提下最小化能耗，并与经典计算机进行能效对比。

Result: 揭示了能耗随量子比特数的缩放规律，量化了量子比特稳定化、门实现和纠错码等关键参数对总能耗的贡献。当系统超过26个量子比特时，在卡诺效率低温系统假设下存在潜在的量子能量优势，且该优势出现在计算优势之前。即使在考虑实际低温系统和控制电子学后，这一优势依然存在。

Conclusion: 量子计算在能效方面可能比经典计算更具优势，且能量优势可能先于计算速度优势出现，为量子计算的实用化提供了新的动力和视角。

Abstract: Quantum computers are emerging as a promising new technology due to their ability to solve complex problems that exceed the capabilities of classical systems in terms of time. Among various implementations, superconducting qubits have become the leading technology due to their scalability and compatibility with quantum error correction mechanisms. Although time has traditionally been the primary focus, energetic efficiency is becoming an increasingly important consideration, especially with the possibility of a quantum energetic advantage. In this article, the energy consumption of the Semiclassical Quantum Fourier Transform was analyzed on a superconducting quantum computing platform based on cat qubits. Quantum error correction mechanisms were studied and considered in the energy estimations. The results show how the energy consumption scales with the number of qubits and how the most relevant parameters required for qubit stabilization, gate implementation, and error correction codes contribute to the overall energy usage. An optimization method was developed to tune these parameters with the goal of minimizing energy consumption while maintaining qubit fidelities above a given threshold. Additionally, a comparative study with state-of-the-art classical computers indicates a potential quantum energetic advantage for systems with more than 26 qubits, assuming cryogenic systems operating at Carnot efficiency, with this energetic advantage arising before any computational advantage. This behavior persists even when realistic cryogenic systems and control electronics are taken into account.
